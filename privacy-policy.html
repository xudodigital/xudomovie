Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

XUDOMovie ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit xudomovie.us.

1. Information We Collect

Locally stored data: XUDOMovie stores your watch history, favorites, and Watch Later list exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymised usage data such as pages visited, session duration, and general geographic location (country level). This helps us improve the service. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Language preference: Your selected language is saved in localStorage under the key xudo_lang.

2. Cookies

We do not set any first-party cookies. Google Analytics may set third-party cookies in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

  • TMDB API: Movie and TV show data is fetched from The Movie Database (TMDB). Please review TMDB's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Flag CDN: Country flag images are loaded from flagcdn.com.

4. Data Retention

All data stored in localStorage remains on your device until you clear your browser storage. We do not retain any personal data on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

XUDOMovie is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
